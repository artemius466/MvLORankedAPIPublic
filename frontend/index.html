RULESET

Version 1.0.0. Effective for all ranked matches

1. Match rules

Coins For Powerup: 8
Custom Powerups: Enabled
Teams: Disabled
Lives: Disabled
Stars To Win: 15
Timer: 10 minutes

2. Maps

There are 4 ranked maps (Grass, Jungle, Bricks, Desert). Once a match is found and both players accept, a randomly picked host bans 2 of them, then the other player bans 1 more — whichever map is left is the one you play.

3. Disconnects

Once you're in a room, you cannot disconnect without losing ELO points. It's scored as a loss for you, if you left the game after accepting a match. Bad or unstable internet is not our problem and we will not revert match results since you can't even prove that it was an internet problem.

4. Rating

Rating is one shared number across every ranked match. We use a modern rating system similar to what other competitive games use nowadays, which looks at more than just wins and losses to figure out how confident it is in your rank. Because of this, newer or less active players can see bigger swings until the system has a good read on their skill, while established players move more gradually. Beating opponents above your rating is worth more than beating ones below it. The tier map lists the rating each rank needs.

5. Fair play

Cheats, or third-party software or hardware that gives a clear unfair edge, exploiting bugs or glitches for an advantage is forbidden

6. Account integrity

Rank manipulation (alt accounts to play below your skill, boosting, or deliberately deranking, account sharing and bots) is forbidden.

7. Match integrity

Match fixing and win trading. Stream sniping off an opponent's live broadcast. Queue dodging.

Any violation of rules 5–7 is a ban. We can see the logs. Please do not test this.

Disputes go in the Discord ticket channel with a match id attached. You can get it at match history.
